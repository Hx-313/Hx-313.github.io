# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential employers and clients evaluating Hafiz Ali Abdullah's software engineering and product-building work.

## Product Purpose

Present Hafiz's software engineering, product design, and SaaS-building work as credible, memorable, and easy to evaluate quickly.

## Brand Personality

Modern, cinematic, intentional.

## Anti-references

Avoid overflow, hidden navigation, blank transition frames, generic SaaS mockups, and motion or decoration that competes with the portfolio content.

## Design Principles

- Make capability legible within the first viewport.
- Use motion and atmosphere to support hierarchy, not spectacle.
- Keep navigation available and stable while the visitor explores.
- Show product thinking through concrete, scannable evidence.
- Preserve accessible contrast, focus, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support `prefers-reduced-motion`, keyboard focus, readable contrast, semantic navigation, and responsive layouts without horizontal overflow.
