## Conservative P0 gate

Block content that promotes illegal/counterfeit goods, sexual exploitation, hateful or violent abuse, scams, privacy violations, or known intellectual-property infringement. Block deceptive product/result imagery and any fabricated offer or endorsement.

Warn for regulated goods, medical/health outcomes, extreme before/after imagery, unclear paid/affiliate relationships, sensitive personal attributes, or synthetic media that could be mistaken for actual product performance. Do not assume organic-post eligibility implies ad eligibility; if the merchant intends to boost the post, apply Meta advertising rules separately.

The preflight is advisory automation. If official rules cannot be retrieved or the category is unclear, use `warning` and require merchant review; never silently convert uncertainty to `pass`.
