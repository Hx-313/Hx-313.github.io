## Conservative P0 gate

Block illegal or counterfeit goods, private information, known IP infringement, scams/deceptive offers, hateful/violent abuse, malicious links, harmful deceptive media, or content intended to manipulate the platform.

Warn for regulated goods, high-risk health/safety claims, synthetic product/result media, repeated near-identical copy, and excessive or irrelevant hashtags. Avoid link-only posts, bulk duplicative content, engagement manipulation, and misleading identity or account behavior.

If official policy retrieval fails or a category is unclear, return `warning` and require merchant review. Uncertainty is never a `pass`.
